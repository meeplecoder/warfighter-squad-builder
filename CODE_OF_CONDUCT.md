# Code of Conduct

This is a friendly hobby project for people who love a card game. We want everyone — newcomers and veterans, whatever
their background — to feel welcome.

**Be kind and constructive.** Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume good intent, give specific feedback, and say
thanks when someone helps.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults or personal attacks, discrimination, sexualised content, publishing other
people's private information, trolling, and spam.

**Keep it on topic and legal.** Don't post material you have no right to share. In particular, do not post DVG's card
text, scans or artwork here (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

**Enforcement.** The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and
may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" or contact the maintainer privately
through the repository's Security tab (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any place where someone represents the
project.
